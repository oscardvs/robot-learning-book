**Count: 30 assigned papers**, three per week for weeks 2–11. Week 1 has no paper
discussion and week 12 is guest lectures only. (The task brief estimated \~33.)

## Week 1 (Feb 16) — Introduction to Robot Learning

No paper discussion. No guest.

## Week 2 (Feb 23) — Robot Control & MDPs

- Mania et al., 2018 — _Simple random search provides a competitive approach to RL_
- Irpan, 2018 — _Deep RL Doesn't Work Yet_
- Pathak et al., 2017 — _Curiosity-driven Exploration by Self-supervised Prediction_

Guest: **Abhishek Gupta** (Prof., University of Washington) — course page spells it
"Abishek".

## Week 3 (Mar 02) — Imitation Learning

- Den Haan et al., 2019 — _Causal Confusion in Imitation Learning_
- Pari et al., 2021 — _The surprising effectiveness of representation learning for visual imitation_
- Zeng et al., 2020 — _Transporter Networks: Rearranging the Visual World for Robotic Manipulation_

Guest: **Danfei Xu** (Prof., Georgia Tech)

## Week 4 (Mar 09) — Reinforcement Learning I

- Salimans et al., 2017 — _Evolution Strategies as a Scalable Alternative to RL_
- Zeng et al., 2018 — _Learning Synergies between Pushing and Grasping_
- Luo et al., 2024 — _Precise and Dexterous Robotic Manipulation via Human-in-the-Loop RL_

Guest: **Aviral Kumar** (Prof., Carnegie Mellon University & Google DeepMind)

## Week 5 (Mar 16) — Reinforcement Learning II

- Levine et al., 2015 — _End-to-End Training of Deep Visuomotor Policies_
- Ma et al., 2023 — _Eureka: Human-Level Reward Design via Coding LLMs_
- Rosete-Beas et al., 2022 — _Latent Plans for Task Agnostic Offline Reinforcement Learning_

Guest: **Andrew Wagenmaker** (Postdoc, UC Berkeley) — _not in the task brief's list
of ten; this makes eleven guest speakers._

## Week 6 (Mar 23) — Generative Models

- Janner & Du et al., 2022 — _Planning with Diffusion for Flexible Behavior Synthesis_
- Florence et al., 2021 — _Implicit Behavioral Cloning_
- Wagenmaker et al., 2025 — _Steering Your Diffusion Policy with Latent Space RL_

Guest: **Cheng Chi** (Co-Founder Sunday Robotics; lead of Diffusion Policy & UMI)

## Week 7 (Mar 30) — Sequence Modeling and Transformers

- Chen et al., 2021 — _Decision Transformer: RL via Sequence Modeling_
- Zhao et al., 2023 — _Learning Fine-Grained Bimanual Manipulation_ (ALOHA)
- Radosavovic et al., 2024 — _Humanoid Locomotion as Next Token Prediction_

Guest: **Ted Xiao** (Co-Founder Prometheus, ex-Google)

## Week 8 (Apr 13) — World Models

- Du et al., 2023 — _Learning Universal Policies via Text-Guided Video Generation_
- Hafner et al., 2025 — _Training Agents Inside of Scalable World Models_
- Ye et al., 2026 — _World Action Models are Zero-shot Policies_

Guest: **Scott Reed** (Principal Research Scientist, NVIDIA GEAR Lab)

## Week 9 (Apr 27) — Generalist Robot Policies

- Lynch et al., 2021 — _Language Conditioned Imitation Learning over Unstructured Data_
- Reed et al., 2022 — _A Generalist Agent_ (Gato)
- Physical Intelligence, 2025 — _π\*0.6: a VLA That Learns From Experience_

Guest: **Quan Vuong** (Co-Founder, Physical Intelligence)

## Week 10 (May 04) — Embodied Reasoning and Test-time Scaling

- Fu et al., 2024 — _In-Context Imitation Learning via Next-Token Prediction_
- Wang et al., 2023 — _VOYAGER: An Open-Ended Embodied Agent with LLMs_
- Chen et al., 2025 — _Training Strategies for Efficient Embodied Reasoning_

Guest: **Archit Sharma** (Research Scientist, Google DeepMind; co-creator of the
Gemini Deep Think series)

## Week 11 (May 11) — Frontier & Open Problems

- LeCun, 2022 — _A Path Towards Autonomous Machine Intelligence_
- Sutton, 2019 — _The Bitter Lesson_
- Brooks, 1991 — _Intelligence without Representation_

Guest: **Lucas Beyer** (Meta Superintelligence Labs)

## Week 12 (May 18) — Guest Lectures only

**Dieter Fox** (Prof., University of Washington & Director, AI2)
