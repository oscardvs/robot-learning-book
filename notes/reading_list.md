# Assigned reading & guest lecturers

Transcribed verbatim from the course page,
<https://cvg.ethz.ch/lectures/Robot-Learning/> (retrieved 2026-07-25).
Titles are as the course page gives them; full citations get resolved in Phase 6.

**Count: 30 assigned papers**, three per week for weeks 2–11. Week 1 has no paper
discussion and week 12 is guest lectures only. (The task brief estimated ~33.)
